§ 01

About my research専門の紹介

I work in behavioral economics, experimental social science, and computational social science. Using experiments as my main method, I study social behavior — cooperation, trust, altruism, and inequality aversion — as well as nudges, the applied face of behavioral economics.

Beyond the laboratory, I run large-scale online experiments on crowdsourcing platforms and field experiments with municipalities and firms, drawing on data from diverse populations to examine cooperation and ethics in human society.

I am the Principal Investigator of BEER Lab (Behavioral and Experimental Economics Research Lab) at the School of Information and Communication, Meiji University, where I lead these research projects.

Recently I have extended this work to the sociality of AI: how socially artificial agents behave, and whether they are steered by dark patterns and nudges in the same way people are, examined through economic game experiments.

§ 02

Research themes研究テーマ

01 / Cooperation

Cooperation and social dilemmas

Public goods & catastrophe games

Using public goods games, catastrophe games, and related economic game experiments to examine the asymmetric effects of loss, disaster, and volatility on altruism and cooperation.

public goodscatastrophealtruism
02 / Trust & Surveillance

Trust under surveillance

Acceptability, well-being, privacy

Experimental work on how surveillance is tolerated in an information society, and how the feeling of being watched affects well-being and social preferences — from CCTV to social media.

surveillancewell-beingprivacy
03 / Human × AI

Sociality of AI and cooperation with humans

Sociality of artificial agents

Crowdsourced experiments with virtual AI agents examine how trust between humans and AI is created, damaged, and restored. A further line asks how far AI itself exhibits "sociality," and whether it is steered by dark patterns and nudges as humans are.

AI socialitytrust dynamicsdark patternsnudge
04 / Nudge

Nudges in the wild

Behavioral interventions with municipalities

Designing and testing nudges and incentive systems that promote recycling and pro-environmental behavior, in partnership with local governments and firms such as the town of Minamisanriku.

nudgefield experimentenvironment
05 / Inequality

Inequality and economic mobility

Wealth visibility & fairness

How the visibility of wealth shapes economic mobility and social preferences, studied through both game experiments and big-data analysis. See Journal of Economic Interaction and Coordination (2025).

wealthmobilityfairness
06 / Methods

Methods for online experiments

Online experiment infrastructure

Building experimental environments around oTree, and studying quality-control problems in crowdsourced experiments such as satisficing and instructional manipulation checks.

oTreecrowdsourcingIMC
